## Notable Design Decisions Made

- Keep `spec-*` classes page-local for now rather than prematurely promoting them.
- Promote only repeated, stable patterns into shared CSS.
- Use reusable primitives plus a limited shared semantic layer.
- Prefer explicit surface context (`.on-light`, `.on-dark`) over trying to infer button contrast from computed background.
